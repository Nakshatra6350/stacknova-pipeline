StackNova Publisher

StackNova Publisher is the personal publishing tool behind the StackNova YouTube channel (@StackNovaDev), an educational channel about backend engineering, distributed systems and system design.

It uploads the channel owner's own videos and Shorts, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and schedules, and reads the channel's own analytics for a weekly report. Every upload is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is made public. It is used only by the channel owner; no one else signs in.
