Privacy policy

StackNova Publisher · last updated 16 October 2026

Who uses it

StackNova Publisher is a personal tool used only by the owner of the StackNova YouTube channel (@StackNovaDev) to manage that channel. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else and does not access any other user's account.

What it accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, to upload the owner's videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publishing schedule.
  • The owner's own channel analytics, through the YouTube Analytics API, to produce a weekly report for the owner.

What it stores

Only the IDs and publishing status of the channel's own videos, and aggregate analytics of the channel, kept in the project's repository for the weekly report. It stores no data about viewers or any other user. Credentials are kept as encrypted repository secrets and are never published.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool's use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the StackNova YouTube channel or the project's GitHub issues.
